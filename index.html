Anambra State

Anambra is a state in southeastern Nigeria, bordered by delta, Imo, Rivers, Enugu, and Kogi States. it is known for its strong commercial activities, industrial development, education, and cultural heritage. The state is a major center for trade and entrepreneurship, particularly in Onitsha and Nnewi. Awka, its capital serves as the administrative center of the state
